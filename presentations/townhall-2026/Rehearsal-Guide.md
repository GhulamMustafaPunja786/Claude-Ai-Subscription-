# ElecSecure Town Hall — Rehearsal Guide
## Focus: Slides 6, 11, and 12 (the 3 most important slides)

These slides carry the most weight. Rehearse them until you can deliver each in your sleep.

---

## Slide 6: "How It Works" (Process + Demo)

**Time target:** 3 minutes  
**Goal:** Make everyone understand the product in plain English

### What the audience must remember
1. The device watches electricity continuously
2. Problems are caught early
3. Users get phone alerts and can act remotely

### Rehearsal script (memorise this flow)

> "ElecSecure works in five steps.  
> **First, Sense** — the device monitors voltage, current, and temperature around the clock.  
> **Second, Detect** — it spots abnormal patterns before they become emergencies.  
> **Third, Alert** — the user gets an instant notification on their phone.  
> **Fourth, Act** — they can investigate or shut off a circuit remotely for safety.  
> **Fifth, Optimise** — over time, the system shows where energy is being wasted.  
>  
> [Optional demo] Let me show you what that looks like in the app..."

### Practice checklist
- [ ] Deliver all 5 steps without reading the slide
- [ ] Run the 90-second demo (see `Live-Demo-Script.md`)
- [ ] Have screenshot backup ready
- [ ] End with: *"Safety, savings, and control — in one platform."*

### Common mistakes to avoid
- Spending too long explaining sensors and microcontrollers
- Skipping the demo entirely (this is your "wow" moment)
- Going over 4 minutes on this slide

## Slide 11: "3-Year Roadmap"

**Time target:** 2–3 minutes  
**Goal:** Show you have a credible, phased plan — not just a dream

### What the audience must remember
- Year 1 = Launch (400 customers)
- Year 2 = Scale + profit (800 customers)
- Year 3 = Leadership (1,500 customers)

### Rehearsal script

> "We have a clear three-year plan — not a vague ambition.  
>  
> **Year 1 is Launch.** We enter the UK market, achieve certification, and onboard our first 400 customers. This is our foundation year.  
>  
> **Year 2 is Scale.** We expand nationwide, reach 800 customers, and become profitable. Subscription revenue starts compounding.  
>  
> **Year 3 is Leadership.** We hit 1,500 customers, strengthen our UK position, and begin international expansion.  
>  
> Each phase builds on the last. We're not trying to do everything at once."

### Practice checklist
- [ ] Pause for 2 seconds after each year
- [ ] Point to each column as you speak
- [ ] Don't rush the customer numbers — they prove traction targets
- [ ] Connect Year 2 profitability to business sustainability

### For non-project audience
Add one line:
> "Even if you're not on this team, these milestones affect hiring, partnerships, and company growth."

## Slide 12: "Financial Outlook"

**Time target:** 2 minutes  
**Goal:** Show the business case is real without overwhelming with numbers

### What the audience must remember
- Revenue grows from £235K → £751K → £1.42M
- Year 1 is investment; profit starts Year 2
- The model is sustainable, not speculative

### Rehearsal script

> "Here's the financial picture at a high level.  
>  
> **Year 1:** £235,000 in revenue. This is our foundation year — we're investing in product, certification, and market entry. A small loss here is expected and planned.  
>  
> **Year 2:** £751,000 in revenue with £154,000 profit after tax. This is where subscription revenue starts to compound alongside device sales.  
>  
> **Year 3:** £1.42 million in revenue with nearly £448,000 profit. This is the payoff of disciplined execution.  
>  
> Our revenue comes from three streams: device sales, monthly subscriptions, and support services. That mix gives us both upfront revenue and long-term recurring income."

### Practice checklist
- [ ] Say numbers slowly and clearly
- [ ] Don't apologise for Year 1 — frame it as planned investment
- [ ] If executives are in the room, offer: *"Detailed assumptions are in the blueprint document."*
- [ ] End with confidence, not hedging

### If someone asks "Are these realistic?"
**Answer:**
> "These are based on our business plan assumptions: 400, 800, and 1,500 customers over three years, with £300 device sales and £49 monthly subscriptions. We're targeting a growing £2.4 billion UK market. Happy to walk through the model in detail after the session."

## Confidence Boosters (day of town hall)

1. **First 30 seconds matter most** — nail your opening line
2. **Eye contact** — scan left, centre, right
3. **Pause after big numbers** — let them land
4. **If you lose your place** — go back to: *"Safety, savings, control"*
5. **End on mission**, not money: *"We're making electricity safer and smarter for everyone."*
