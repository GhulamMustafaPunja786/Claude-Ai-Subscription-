**Annual Town Hall 2026 | Azfar Mushtaq, Managing Director**

## What is ElecSecure?
A smart electrical safety and energy management platform combining:
- **Hardware device** installed in the electrical system
- **Mobile app** for monitoring, alerts, and remote control
- **Cloud platform** for analytics and optimisation

---

## The Problem
- Electrical faults are often detected too late
- Energy waste is invisible without real-time data
- Smart homes lack integrated electrical protection
- Businesses face downtime and safety risk

---

## Our Solution
| Capability | Benefit |
|------------|---------|
| Real-time fault detection | Prevents fires and system damage |
| Energy consumption insights | Reduces bills and waste |
| Remote monitoring & control | Convenience from anywhere |
| Smart home integration | Unified automation experience |
