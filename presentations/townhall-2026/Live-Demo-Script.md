# ElecSecure — Live Demo Script (90 Seconds)
## For Town Hall Presentation | Slide: "How It Works"

Use this script during or immediately after the "How It Works" slide.  
If you cannot do a live demo, narrate this as a **story** — it still works powerfully.

---

## Setup (before town hall)

- [ ] Phone charged and app logged in (demo account)
- [ ] Demo dashboard showing a sample home/building
- [ ] Pre-stage one alert notification (or use demo mode)
- [ ] Screen mirroring tested (HDMI / AirPlay / Teams share)
- [ ] Backup: 3 screenshots on laptop if live app fails

---

## The 90-Second Script

### 0:00–0:15 | Open the dashboard
**Say:**
> "Let me show you what a user sees. This is the ElecSecure mobile dashboard. At a glance, they can see system health, energy use, and any active warnings."

**Do:** Open app → show home screen with green "System Healthy" status.

---

### 0:15–0:35 | Show real-time monitoring
**Say:**
> "Here we can see live electrical data — which circuits are active, how much energy is being used, and whether power quality is normal. This is like a fitness tracker, but for your building's electricity."

**Do:** Tap into energy view → show consumption chart → point to one circuit.

---

### 0:35–0:55 | Trigger or show an alert
**Say:**
> "Now imagine a fault starts developing — maybe an overload or abnormal pattern. ElecSecure detects it immediately and sends an alert to the user's phone. They don't have to wait until something burns out or trips manually."

**Do:** Show notification OR open Alerts screen with a sample warning:
- *"Circuit 3: Elevated load detected — review recommended"*

---

### 0:55–1:15 | Demonstrate remote action
**Say:**
> "From here, the user can take action remotely — view details, acknowledge the alert, or even trip the circuit for safety. That means protection even when they're not on site."

**Do:** Tap alert → show details → tap "Remote Trip" or "Acknowledge" (demo mode).

---

### 1:15–1:30 | Close with value
**Say:**
> "And over time, the system learns usage patterns and recommends ways to save energy. That's the full loop: **Sense, Detect, Alert, Act, Optimise** — safety and savings in one platform."

**Do:** Return to dashboard → pause on summary screen.

---

## If Live Demo Fails (backup narration)

**Say:**
> "The live connection isn't available right now, but here's what users experience: a real-time dashboard, instant alerts when something looks wrong, and the ability to act from their phone — whether they're at home, at work, or halfway around the world."

Then show 2–3 screenshots (dashboard, alert, energy chart).

---

## Demo Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Keep it under 90 seconds | Walk through every menu |
| Speak to outcomes (safety, savings) | Use technical jargon (IoT, arc fault, etc.) |
| Have screenshots as backup | Apologise repeatedly if tech fails |
| Practice twice before town hall | Demo on untested Wi-Fi |

---

## Optional: Audience Participation Line

After the demo, add:
> "If anyone here manages buildings, facilities, or homes — imagine getting this alert before a problem becomes an emergency. That's what we're building."

This helps non-project attendees connect personally.
