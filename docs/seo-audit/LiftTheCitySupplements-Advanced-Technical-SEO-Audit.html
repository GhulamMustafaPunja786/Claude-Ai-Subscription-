SIMPLE VERDICT

This is a commercially healthy Shopify store with a real local brand (Toronto) and a solid technical base. It is NOT losing because Google cannot crawl it. It is losing because collection/page SEO is nearly empty, local SEO is incomplete, the blog is unused, and too many thin/irrelevant collections are publicly indexable. Fixing titles/metas + cleaning collections + adding LocalBusiness/About/FAQ/blog will unlock the biggest gains.

GTIN problem explained simply

Each product flavour/size should have its own barcode (GTIN/UPC). Right now the store often repeats ONE barcode across many variants. Google and shopping feeds treat that as bad data. Fix in Shopify: Products → variant → Barcode field. If you do not know the real barcode, leave it blank.
09_GLOSSARY_EASY_WORDS

Glossary — SEO words in plain English

TermPlain English
Technical SEOMaking sure Google can find, understand, and trust your pages (not just writing blog posts).
Crawl budgetHow much time Google spends scanning your site. Don't waste it on empty/junk pages.
Index / IndexationWhether a page can appear in Google results.
noindexA tag that tells Google 'do not show this page in search'.
CanonicalThe 'main' URL version you want Google to count when duplicates exist.
Meta descriptionThe short blurb under your blue title in Google. Does not directly rank, but boosts clicks.
Title tagThe clickable blue headline in Google. Very important.
H1The main visible page heading. Should match the topic.
Schema / JSON-LDSpecial code that explains products, store info, reviews to Google for rich results.
GTIN / UPC / BarcodeProduct identifier number. Must be unique and correct per variant.
Rich resultsFancy Google listings (stars, price, availability).
Core Web Vitals / CWVGoogle's speed & stability measures (LCP, INP, CLS).
TTFBHow fast the server starts sending the page.
NAPName, Address, Phone — must be consistent for local SEO.
LocalBusiness schemaCode that tells Google your physical store details.
Thin contentPages with little unique value (empty collections, empty blog).
CannibalizationMultiple pages fighting for the same keyword (e.g., generic /products/pre-workout).
CTRClick-through rate — % of searchers who click your result.
E-E-A-TExperience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust — helped by About, real store info, reviews.
SitemapA map of URLs you want Google to discover.
robots.txtRules for bots about where they may crawl (not a full substitute for noindex).
