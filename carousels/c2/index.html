83% of associates who leave do it within five years.

Four things that actually keep them.

NALP Foundation, Associate Attrition CY2024

01

Tell them what the path is.

Most juniors don't leave for money. They leave because nobody said what year three looks like.

02

Give them files, not tasks.

Research and redlines don't build a lawyer. Owning something start to finish does.

03

Review the work, not just the output.

Fifteen minutes of 'here's why I changed this' beats any retention bonus.

04

Pay before they ask.

By the time someone raises it, they already have a number from somewhere else.

Retention isn't an HR problem.

It's the cheapest growth you'll ever buy.
