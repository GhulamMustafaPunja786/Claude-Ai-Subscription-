Exhibit list

Small firms don't stay small because of the market.

Four reasons, and none of them are about being a better lawyer.

Exhibit 01

The owner is the bottleneck.

Every decision routes through one person. That person is also trying to bill.

Exhibit 02

There's no intake system. Just whoever picks up.

The lead that converts is the one called back first. Usually not yours.

Exhibit 03

The best juniors leave the moment they're useful.

You paid to train them. The firm down the street pays to keep them.

Exhibit 04

Nobody owns growth.

Everyone's job, so nobody's job, so it doesn't happen.

None of this is a lawyer problem.

It's a structure problem.
