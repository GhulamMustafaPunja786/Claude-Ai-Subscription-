# Posting guide

What to export, and what each network expects.

## Sizes

| Size in the app | Pixels | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 | Instagram and LinkedIn. Takes the most screen space in the feed. |
| Square 1:1 | 1080 x 1080 | Instagram when you also want a tidy grid. |
| Story 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | Stories and Reels covers. |
| Landscape 4:3 | 1200 x 900 | LinkedIn documents that people read on a laptop. |

Keep every slide in one carousel the same size. Mixed sizes get cropped.

## Instagram

1. Export **ZIP of PNGs** and unzip it. Files are numbered `01`, `02`, `03` so the order is already correct.
2. Move the PNGs to your phone (AirDrop, Google Photos, Drive, email to yourself).
3. In Instagram: new post, tap the multi-select icon, then tap the images **in order**.
4. Paste `caption.txt` from the ZIP as the caption.

Notes:

- Up to 20 images per carousel. Six to ten performs best for teaching content.
- Instagram's interface covers roughly the outer 90 px of a 1080 wide slide. Turn on **Show safe-area guide** to see the margin the app already reserves.
- The first slide is also the grid thumbnail. Instagram crops the grid preview to a square, so keep the hook away from the very top and bottom.

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn carousels are **documents**, not images, so export the **PDF**.

1. Export **PDF (LinkedIn)**.
2. Start a post, choose **Add a document**, upload the PDF.
3. Give the document a title — LinkedIn shows it above the carousel, so make it a benefit ("6 Shopify SEO fixes"), not a file name.
4. Paste your caption in the post body.

Notes:

- Up to 300 pages, but 5 to 12 is the sweet spot.
- Portrait 4:5 and Landscape 4:3 both work. Portrait fills more of a phone screen; landscape reads better on desktop.
- Each page of the PDF is a full-bleed JPEG at your chosen pixel size, so text stays crisp when LinkedIn re-renders it.

## Facebook, X and TikTok

Use the **ZIP of PNGs**. Facebook and X take the images as a normal multi-image post; TikTok takes them as a photo carousel.

## A structure that works

| Slide | Job |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The hook. A promise or a problem, under ten words. |
| 2 | Why it matters, or the list of what is coming. |
| 3 to 6 | One idea per slide. Do not put two ideas on one slide. |
| 7 | A number, a quote or a mistake to avoid — something to break the rhythm. |
| Last | One ask: comment, save or follow. |

Practical habits:

- Read only the headlines top to bottom. If that alone tells the story, the carousel works.
- Repeat the hook's promise on the last slide before the ask.
- Put the same handle and logo on every slide. Screenshots travel.
- Keep the caption short and put the detail on the slides.
